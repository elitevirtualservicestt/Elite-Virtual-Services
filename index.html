The Founder's Story

Hi, I'm Yanz — Your Trusted Partner in Simplifying Business Finances.

I never planned on becoming a bookkeeper. My journey began with an Associate Degree in Culinary Management — a field driven by creativity, precision, and an eye for detail. But as I stepped further into the world of business, I discovered a deep passion for something many entrepreneurs dread: the numbers.

Over the years, I built a strong background in Logistics and Bookkeeping, working alongside businesses of all sizes and gaining an up-close look at their financial challenges. What I saw again and again was the same story — talented, hardworking business owners drowning in receipts, confused by software, and losing sleep over finances they just couldn't get organized.

As a small business owner myself, that story hit close to home. I know the pressure of wearing every hat in your company. I know how hard it is to chase invoices, serve clients, manage staff, and somehow keep your books up to date — all at the same time.

That's why I created Elite Virtual Services. My mission is simple: give entrepreneurs the accurate financial records, practical tools, and peace of mind they need to grow their businesses with confidence.

I specialize in bookkeeping, payroll processing, and QuickBooks and Zoho Books setup — all tailored specifically to the realities of small and medium-sized businesses, whether you're down the street or across the globe.
